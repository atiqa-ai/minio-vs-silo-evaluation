-- why the image variant was settled by evidence --
  The in-use image layer history contains:
    COPY /go/bin/mcli /usr/bin/mcli
    chmod +x /usr/bin/silo /usr/bin/mcli /usr/bin/docker-entrypoint.sh
    ln -sf mcli /usr/bin/mc
    COPY /go/bin/curl /usr/bin/curl
  i.e. the classic variant, with a shell, curl and a client. The
  -distroless variant ships the binary alone with no shell, so it cannot
  support this lab's shell entrypoint or in-container client workflow.

-- fairness consequence --
  The image symlinks its own mcli AS mc. Driving Silo with mcli while
  driving MinIO with mc would mean two different clients and would break
  the Identical-Test Rule. That is why mc is primary for both products.
  Behaviour that differs under mc is a DEV-912 compatibility finding.
